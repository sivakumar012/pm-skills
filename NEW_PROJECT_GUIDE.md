# New Project Lifecycle Guide: PM Skills & Hooks

This document provides a structured end-to-end guide on which PM skills and commands (hooks) to use when building a **new project** from scratch, utilizing the PM Skills Marketplace framework.

---

## Phase 1: Product Discovery & Ideation
Start here to validate the problem, brainstorm solutions, and map out risky assumptions before building anything.

### Key Commands (Hooks)
* `/discover` — Runs the full discovery cycle (ideation → assumption mapping → prioritization → experiments).
* `/interview prep` — Generates a structured customer interview script for early validation.
* `/setup-metrics` — Designs an early product metrics dashboard to know what success looks like.

### Underlying Skills
* `brainstorm-ideas-new` — Ideation focused on new products.
* `identify-assumptions-new` — Maps out GTM, Strategy, Team, and Viability assumptions for startups/new products.
* `prioritize-assumptions` — Ranks assumptions by Impact vs. Risk.
* `brainstorm-experiments-new` — Designs lean startup pretotypes (e.g., fake door tests).
* `opportunity-solution-tree` — Maps outcomes to opportunities and solutions visually.

---

## Phase 2: Market Research & Competitive Analysis
Understand your target audience, size the market, and evaluate competitors.

### Key Commands (Hooks)
* `/research-users` — Builds personas, segments users, and maps out the customer journey.
* `/competitive-analysis` — Analyzes the competitive landscape.

### Underlying Skills
* `market-segments` — Identifies 3–5 initial customer segments with JTBD (Jobs-to-be-Done).
* `user-personas` — Creates refined user personas.
* `market-sizing` — Calculates TAM, SAM, and SOM.
* `competitor-analysis` — Maps out competitor strengths, weaknesses, and differentiation.
* `customer-journey-map` — Maps the end-to-end journey from awareness to retention.

---

## Phase 3: Product Strategy & Business Model
Define the vision, how the product creates value, and how it will make money.

### Key Commands (Hooks)
* `/strategy` — Generates a comprehensive 9-section Product Strategy Canvas.
* `/business-model startup` — Drafts the business model specifically tailored for a new product.
* `/value-proposition` — Designs the 6-part JTBD value proposition.
* `/pricing` — Designs the initial pricing strategy.

### Underlying Skills
* `product-vision` — Crafts an inspiring and achievable product vision.
* `startup-canvas` or `lean-canvas` — Business modeling optimized for new and early-stage products.
* `value-proposition` — Clarifies the Who, Why, Before, How, After, and Alternatives.
* `pricing-strategy` — Defines pricing models and willingness-to-pay.
* `swot-analysis` / `pestle-analysis` — Macro environment checks.

---

## Phase 4: Go-To-Market (GTM) & Growth
Plan how you will acquire your first users and build sustainable growth loops.

### Key Commands (Hooks)
* `/plan-launch` — Creates a full GTM strategy from beachhead to launch day.
* `/growth-strategy` — Designs growth loops and evaluates GTM motions (e.g., Product-led vs. Sales-led).
* `/market-product` — Brainstorms marketing ideas, positioning, and product names.
* `/north-star` — Defines the North Star Metric.

### Underlying Skills
* `beachhead-segment` — Narrows down to the first highly-targeted market segment.
* `ideal-customer-profile` (ICP) — Defines exactly who you are selling to first.
* `product-name` — Helps brainstorm a brand and product name.
* `positioning-ideas` — Differentiates your new product from existing alternatives.
* `growth-loops` — Designs acquisition and retention flywheels.

---

## Phase 5: Execution & Delivery
Translate the strategy into actionable PRDs, tickets, and sprint plans for the engineering team.

### Key Commands (Hooks)
* `/write-prd` — Converts your idea and strategy into a comprehensive PRD.
* `/pre-mortem` — Conducts a risk analysis before kicking off development.
* `/write-stories` — Breaks down features into manageable backlog items.
* `/sprint plan` — Helps organize the first development sprint.

### Underlying Skills
* `create-prd` — Standardized 8-section PRD.
* `user-stories` or `job-stories` — Defines engineering tickets with Acceptance Criteria.
* `prioritization-frameworks` — Helps select which features make the MVP (e.g., MoSCoW, ICE).
* `test-scenarios` — Outlines happy paths and edge cases for QA.

---

## Phase 6: Post-Launch Analytics & Iteration
Measure success and iterate based on real user data.

### Key Commands (Hooks)
* `/analyze-cohorts` — Analyzes user retention for early adopters.
* `/analyze-feedback` — Extracts sentiment and feature requests from initial users.
* `/write-query` — Helps write SQL to fetch product usage data.

### Underlying Skills
* `cohort-analysis` — Measures if your product is sticky.
* `sentiment-analysis` — Understands early user feedback.
* `ab-test-analysis` — Prepares you for future experimentation.
